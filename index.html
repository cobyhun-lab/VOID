📋 PATCH NOTES
v13.1 — INTERFACE — CURRENT
🖥 Tactical Array redesign — the entire interface rebuilt on one design system: phosphor teal on void, a single terminal typeface, zero rounded corners, hairline rules. Every screen now speaks the same language.
◤ Target-lock framing — selecting a hull, loadout, shop card or level-up choice now snaps corner brackets onto it, like the ship's computer acquiring a lock. Watch for it everywhere.
🧹 Interface debt cleared — five dead screens removed, duplicate style generations purged, and six interface elements that had silently lost their styling (shop stat pills, installed badges, the settings volume sliders) restored.
📐 Unified modals — pause, bug report, daily challenge, end-run and unlock dialogs now share one bracketed terminal shell.
♿ Reduced motion respected — all interface animation honours your system's reduced-motion setting. Keyboard focus is now visible.
v13.0 — ARSENAL
⬆ Weapon levels — your stance pair is locked at launch and now levels 1→6 instead of being swapped out. Levels 3 and 6 are behaviour milestones: pierce rounds, twin torpedo tubes, split beams, extra orbitals. Buy levels in the shop, or take them free on level-up.
⚙ Passive systems — 4 slots, 5 levels each, and every passive works BOTH stances: Reactor Cells make Red drain slower AND Blue recharge faster. Choose your four and commit. The old infinite perk stacking is gone — power has a shape now.
⬡ Level-up choices — leveling now pauses the fight and offers 3 picks: free weapon levels, free passive levels, or field repairs. Vampire-style. The every-3rd-wave draft retired in its favour.
⭐ Evolutions — max a weapon (LV6) and its paired passive (LV5), then kill a boss: it drops an Evolution Core. ION TEMPEST. MASS ACCELERATOR. EVENT HORIZON. Eight total — one per stance weapon. Fly into the star to claim it.
🔧 The Workshop — permanent pilot progression in the Hangar. Every run earns Workshop Credits (score ÷ 400 + waves ×2). Spend them on starting hull, Tibanna contracts, tuned thrusters, or a field-kit EMP.
🏦 Tibanna banking — unspent gas converts to score at 20:1 when the run ends. Ending rich is now a strategy.
🐛 Resume fix — resuming a saved run no longer re-applies your hull's passive trait on top of saved stats (Viper pilots were losing 25% hull twice).
v12.8 — ANOMALIES II
☁ Nebula Bank (sector 8+) — drifting clouds hide enemies inside them beyond 130px. All projectiles fly 25% slower in the cloud — and while YOU hide in it, enemy aim goes wide. Hunt or be hunted.
☀ Solar Flare (sector 12+) — a warning line flashes for 2 seconds, then a beam sweeps the sector. Heavy shield damage if you're caught — or herd the wave into the lane and let the sun do your work.
◎ Repulsor Anomaly (sector 14+) — an inverted well pushes everything outward. Homing fire curves wide around it. No camping in this sector.
☄ Comet Flyby (sector 15+) — telegraphed flybys at 900px/s, leaving a burning trail that damages BOTH sides for seconds. Kite enemies through the fire.
✸ Magnetic Minefield (sector 16+) — neutral proximity mines that detonate on anyone. They blink faster when something gets close. Lure the wave through them.
∞ Wormhole Pair (sector 18+) — two linked rings. Anything entering one exits the other: you, enemies, and every bullet. Trick shots are now a build.
⚠⚠ Dual anomalies (sector 20+) — 40% of anomaly sectors roll a second, compatible anomaly. Black hole + ion storm is real and it is glorious.
v12.7 — ANOMALIES
🪨 Asteroid Field (sector 5+) — drifting rocks block fire from BOTH sides. Use them as cover. Collisions hit shields first, hull only if shields are down. Shoot them open for Tibanna — but the amber ones are volatile and detonate in a damaging blast.
🛰 Derelict Wreckage (sector 6+) — crack the hulks open for guaranteed salvage: an EMP charge or a fat Tibanna cache. One in four isn't empty.
🌩 Ion Storm (sector 8+) — inside the cloud, RED stance drains energy twice as fast but BLUE recharges 1.5×. Half of all enemy fire fizzles inside. Blue builds live in it; Red builds fight around it.
🌑 Black Hole (sector 10+) — pulls you, enemies, scraps and every bullet on screen (watch your shots curve). Enemies that cross the event horizon are consumed — no score, no drops. After 20 seconds it collapses in a shockwave that stuns everything nearby. Time your wave around it.
📋 Anomaly intel — incoming anomalies are announced at wave start and shown next to the wave modifier in the bottom status band (which is now actually wired up — it never displayed before).
◈ Daily fairness — on daily challenge runs, anomalies are seeded from the date: everyone faces identical sectors, rock for rock.
v12.6 — HANGAR & PERFORMANCE
🛠 The Hangar — pilot, hull and loadout now live on one screen. Your last setup is pre-selected: returning pilots launch in two taps. Callsign is asked once, ever — edit it with the ✎ next to your name.
⚡ Quick Launch — new title button. Skips every menu and launches your last hull + loadout instantly.
🚀 Mobile performance overhaul — all in-combat glow is now pre-rendered instead of computed per frame, HUD updates were cut 6×, and per-frame memory churn eliminated. Phones should hold a steady framerate even in dense waves.
📱 Performance Mode — Settings → Display. Trims particles and starfield density. On by default on phones, switchable any time.
🐛 Daily challenge fix — the audio system was silently stripping the daily flag from runs launched after sound init. Daily runs now always register as daily.
🐛 Hull trait fix — runs started without visiting ship select (daily, retry) now correctly apply your hull's passive trait instead of skipping it.
🔁 Try Again fixed — restarting from the debrief now reuses the loadout you just played instead of resetting to Raider.
v9.1 — STANCE SYSTEM
⚔️ Stance System — choose a Loadout at run start. Each has a Red (attack) and Blue (control) weapon. Toggle mid-combat with F/Shift or the mobile Stance button. Red drains energy; Blue recharges it.
🎯 Four Loadouts — RAIDER (twin cannons + strafe burst), SENTINEL (pierce rifle + shield pulse), WARDEN (homing torpedo + siege mines), PHANTOM (sustained beam + gravity orbital).
💣 Warden reworked — Red stance now fires a tracking Proton Torpedo. Blue stance deploys a Siege Array: 3 proximity mines in formation, each triggering a 3-stage cluster detonation. Upgrades to 4 and 8 mines.
🛒 Shop reworked — weapons split into Red and Blue upgrade paths per loadout. All mines now cluster-detonate in 3 cascading stages.
🐛 Fixes — loadout selection now correctly applied on game start (audio wrapper was dropping the argument). Blue stance shop upgrades now route correctly. Tutorial progression fixed.
v8.3
🗄 Cloudflare D1 database — leaderboard migrated from JSONBin to D1 (SQLite at the edge). Proper concurrent writes, no race conditions, no external dependency.
🐛 End run bug fixed — game loop now fully stops when ending a run. No longer possible to return to menu and continue playing via the shop.
⚡ Performance improvements — particles capped at 120, damage floaters at 40, enemy bullets at 200 with off-screen culling. Canvas draw skipped entirely on menus and game over screen.
⚖️ Difficulty rebalance — enemy HP scaling reduced (1.15→1.10 per wave), bullet damage scaling reduced (8%→5% per wave), spawn counts trimmed, scrap drops increased for tier 1 & 2 enemies.
🛡 Screen boundary AI — all enemies now repelled from screen edges. No more units drifting off-screen on mobile.
⌨️ Bug report typing fixed — spacebar and other keys now work correctly in the report textarea.
🏆 Rank 0 bug fixed — leaderboard rank now always resolves correctly after score submission.
v8.1 — SECURITY & FIXES
🔐 Cloudflare Worker backend — API key removed from client. Leaderboard now proxied through a secure Worker with server-side validation.
🐛 Bug report system — pause menu → Report Bug. Captures wave, score, kills, weapon loadout, browser and version. Reports land in Discord instantly.
🏆 Leaderboard top 10 — only top 10 shown. Your rank displayed below if outside top 10. Version number shown on every entry.
🐛 END button fixed — confirm modal was hidden behind the shop overlay. Z-index corrected.
🐛 Crosshair crash fixed — stale shake offset variables removed from draw call.
v8.0 — CONTENT EXPANSION
🧠 Enemy AI overhaul — 6 distinct steering behaviours: Raiders flock, Bombers orbit, Gunships flank and switch sides, Interceptors make attack runs, Carriers hold position and deploy fighters.
👾 2 new enemy types — Interceptor (fast strafing attack runs, tier 2) and Carrier (slow, spawns TIE fighters every 5 seconds, tier 3).
💀 Boss every 10 waves — 3 escalating tiers. Each boss has 3 AI phases: orbit → charge → erratic. Fire patterns escalate from spread to rotating ring burst to fast aimed shots.
🌿 Beam Fork — projectile splits into a Y on first enemy hit [3×22 dmg].
💣 Mine Detonator — drops armed proximity mines behind you. 90 dmg AOE, 12 second lifespan.
⚓ Gravity Anchor — fires a projectile that plants a gravity well, pulling all nearby enemies in for 1.8 seconds.
🔥 Pulse Lance — fast pierce bolt that detonates an 80px AOE shockwave on hit [45 dmg + splash].
